## Description (≤4000 characters)

VeganLens helps you make more informed choices while shopping. Scan a barcode or enter it manually and get a clear result: vegan-friendly, not vegan-friendly, or uncertain.

Browse products from Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, Open Product Facts, and Open Pet Food Facts. Review ingredients, additives and E-numbers, allergens, and nutrition. Ingredients are shown in your configured app language, with on-device translation when the label is in another language.

Allergens at a glance:

* Check the product's declared allergens, including the 14 subject to mandatory declaration in the European Union: gluten, crustaceans, eggs, fish, peanuts, soya beans, milk, nuts, celery, mustard, sesame seeds, sulphur dioxide and sulphites, lupin, and molluscs.
* Mark the ones you want to monitor in your profile and VeganLens highlights them with a prominent warning in every result.
* Enable strict mode to also receive warnings about traces and related possible matches.
* This information comes from open data contributed by the community and may be incomplete or out of date: always check the product packaging before consuming a product.

Photograph the ingredient list:

* When a product is not in the databases, photograph its ingredient list and VeganLens analyzes it on the device.
* The text is recognized with Vision without leaving the device: the image is not uploaded or stored for analysis.
* It recognizes labels in Spanish, English, German, French, Italian, and Portuguese, locates the ingredient list within the label, and selects your language block on multilingual packaging.
* The verdict is conservative: it only says “vegan-friendly” when it recognizes every ingredient and, if something is not recognized, asks you to review it rather than take a risk.
* You can correct the recognized text manually and analyze it again.

Make the analysis fit your needs:

* Add additives or ingredient words to custom watchlists.
* Get warnings when a product contains something you want to monitor.
* Animal-derived additives mark the product as not vegan-friendly and are shown as the reason for the verdict.
* The verdict explains which ingredient it is based on and whether it comes from Open Food Facts data or a detection in the text.
* Find vegan-friendly alternatives from the same category when a result is not vegan-friendly or uncertain.
* Receive palm-oil warnings for products that contain it.

You also get:

* An offline cache that keeps consulted products for 14 days.
* History and favourites stored locally on your device.
* A scanning widget on the home screen and quick access to the scanner.
* Share the result with the product image and the Open Food Facts link.
* Accessibility settings and VoiceOver support.
* Contributions to the Open Facts databases when you choose to submit them, with product data and optional photos, published through the app's account. No personal account or login is required.
* A user interface in Spanish, English, German, French, Italian, and Portuguese.

Privacy first: no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or trackers. No personal account, credentials, or personal identifiers are required. Your preferences, history, favourites, and cache stay on your device. Contributions are sent only when you actively start them, to the Open Facts database you select. VeganLens is free and powered by open data.
