## Description (≤4000 characters)

VeganLens helps you make more informed choices while shopping. Scan a barcode or enter it manually and get a clear result: vegan-friendly, not vegan-friendly, or uncertain.

Browse products from Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, Open Product Facts, and Open Pet Food Facts. Review ingredients, additives and E-numbers, allergens, and nutrition. Ingredients are shown in your configured app language.

Make the analysis fit your needs:

* Configure your allergen profile and enable strict mode.
* Add additives or ingredient words to custom watchlists.
* Get warnings when a product contains something you want to monitor.
* Find vegan-friendly alternatives from the same category when a result is not vegan-friendly or uncertain.

You also get:

* An offline cache for recently scanned products.
* History and favourites stored locally on your device.
* On-device ingredient OCR with Vision: capture a list and fill the ingredients field. The image is not uploaded or stored for OCR.
* Anonymous contributions to the Open Facts databases only when you choose to start them, with product data and optional photos. No account or login is required.
* A user interface in Spanish, English, German, and French.

Privacy first: no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, trackers, accounts, or personal identifiers. VeganLens is free and powered by open data.
