## Introduction

The Local References Section is a place where different companies have the opportunity to reference or detail customized information specific to them.

## Responsibility

The documentation in this section is not part of the Gluon Product. Each company is responsible for the content it publishes, whether at a global level or on a specific page, ensuring the information is correctly presented.

## Structure

Usually, the following sections will be represented under Local References:

* **Local Processes**: Details on internal processes, with complete documentation or links to external references.
* **Local Components**: Documentation on Component Templates developed by the company's DevOps or shared by other companies and made available through Gluon Component Catalog.
* **Local Utilities**: Documentation on utilities created by the company's DevOps, including but not limited to scripts, tools, and software enhancements.
