Unlock the Power of Gluon with Bite-sized Knowledge Pills.

The main goal of this section is to help enhance developer experience, starting with making the use of Gluon as seamless as possible.
That's why we've created Gluon Essentials, a series of 2-4 minute video guides designed to get into the most important aspects of our platform.

- **Effortless Learning:** our videos deliver knowledge directly to you in a quick, digestible format without having to dive through long pages of documentation.
- **Topic Expertise:** dive into specific subjects, from API Governance to Onboarding or API Catalog.
- **Save Time:** time is valuable. Instead of hours, you will only need minutes to go over Gluon's most critical aspects.
- **On-Demand Access:** Gluon Essentials are available whenever you need them, so you can learn at your own pace.
