### Current Situation

Previously, Release Management configurations at a company level, allowed activating/inactivating release production deployments at company level, which affected all applications within that company.

### What's the Improvement?

With this new improvement, Release Management can now be enabled or disabled at the application level.
 This means that company owners can choose to activate or deactivate release processes for specific applications within the organization, providing greater flexibility and control over how releases are managed across different applications.
