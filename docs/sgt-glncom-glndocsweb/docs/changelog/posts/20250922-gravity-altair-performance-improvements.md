### What's New?

Gluon Gravity Altair workflows are now prepared for managing large Changeman expedients as well as improves on performance for Sonar CobolMicrofocus stages.

### Why is this important?

With these new enhancements, on one hand large packages may be handle by the system minimizing errors and from the other hand native developers may not wait for so long to get workflows executions finish and then get  their quality reports available.
