### What's Changing?

We are decommissioning the AFE framework as a globally and centrally maintained solution from Gluon.

Going forward, the AFE framework—including its component templates, libraries, documentation, and bug resolution—will be maintained and evolved locally by the Brazil team.

As part of this transition, the documentation for the AFE framework will be removed, and the AFE SPA and AFE Microfront templates will be hidden for all users outside of Brazil.

### Why Is This Important?

This change allows more focused support of the AFE framework tailored to the specific needs of the Brazil region.

For users outside Brazil, this means the AFE framework will no longer be available or supported, so we encourage teams to plan their projects accordingly and migrate to alternative solutions if necessary.
