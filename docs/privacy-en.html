VeganCheck Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

VeganCheck (bundle identifier com.ralvarezmar.veganchecker) is an iOS app that scans product barcodes and retrieves public product information to show ingredients, additives, allergens, nutrition information, and an indicative analysis of whether a product is suitable for a vegan diet. The developer does not operate a backend server for the app.

Summary

VeganCheck does not collect personal data for the developer and does not require an account, login, or credentials. The current app includes no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or third-party trackers.

Data stored on the device

The app stores the following only on your device:

  • Local scan history: barcode, product name, brand, image URL, and scan date.
  • Favorites: barcode, product name, brand, image URL, and the date saved.
  • A local cache of queried products, including the source and cache date, to support later offline lookups. The cache is bounded and managed automatically.
  • Local preferences such as selected allergens, strict mode, and whether onboarding has been seen.

These data are not sent to the developer. You can clear scan history and remove favorites in the app. The local cache is removed when VeganCheck is uninstalled. Uninstalling the app removes its local data from the device.

Camera and barcodes

VeganCheck requests camera access only to read product barcodes. Analysis runs locally on the device using AVFoundation. Camera frames are processed in memory: no photos or videos are taken, stored, or sent to any server.

Queries to third-party databases

When you scan or search for a product, the app may connect to these public databases in the Open Food Facts family:

  • Open Food Facts
  • Open Beauty Facts
  • Open Product Facts
  • Open Pet Food Facts

The app sends the barcode or search terms needed to locate the product. Product images may be loaded from servers or CDNs named by those databases. Names, email addresses, and other personal identifiers are not sent.

These queries are governed by the terms and privacy policies of the relevant services. In particular, see Open Food Facts' privacy policy: world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

Analytics, crash reporting, advertising, and tracking

  • Usage analytics.
  • Crash reports or crash-reporting services.
  • Advertising or advertising-network SDKs.
  • Third-party trackers.

Retention and security

Local data remain on the device until you clear them in the app, remove a favorite, the cache is replaced or removed, or you uninstall VeganCheck. Queries to third-party databases are subject to their own policies and retention periods.

iOS app sandboxing protects local storage from other apps. VeganCheck does not store credentials or transmit data to the developer. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security; keep iOS updated and download the app from trusted sources.

Children

VeganCheck is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect children's personal data. No account is required.

Rights and how to exercise them

You can control local data by clearing history, removing favorites, or uninstalling VeganCheck. To exercise rights over data processed by a third-party database, contact that database under its own privacy policy. The developer does not operate a backend or user account through which local data can be identified or managed.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if VeganCheck features or its services change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated date.
